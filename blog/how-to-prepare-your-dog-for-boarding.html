How to Prepare Your Dog for Boarding

Essential tips to ensure a calm and stress-free stay for your pet.

Preparing your dog for boarding is an important step to ensure a smooth and stress-free experience. At House & Pets Care, we strive to maintain your pet’s routine and provide a calm, home-style environment. Bringing familiar items and sharing essential information helps your dog feel comfortable and secure while you are away.

What to Bring for Your Dog’s Boarding Stay

Providing the right items allows us to recreate your dog’s usual environment and minimize anxiety. Here are the essentials we recommend bringing:

  • Food: Bring your dog’s regular food in the appropriate quantity for their stay to avoid digestive issues and maintain consistency.
  • Food and Water Bowls: If your dog is used to specific bowls, bringing them helps maintain their routine and comfort.
  • Blanket or Favorite Item: A blanket, toy, or personal item that smells like home can significantly reduce anxiety and provide reassurance.
  • Medications: If your dog requires medication, please provide clear written instructions including dosage and administration times.
  • Veterinarian Contact Information: Sharing your veterinarian’s details allows us to respond quickly in case of an emergency.
  • Daily Routine Summary: To help maintain consistency, please provide information about:
    • Feeding schedule
    • Exercise and walking routine
    • Medication times (if applicable)
    • Playtime preferences
    • Sleeping habits
    • Behavioral notes or special needs
  • Music or Comfort Sounds: If your dog is accustomed to specific music or background sounds, let us know so we can recreate a familiar and calming environment.

Tips for a Stress-Free Boarding Experience

  • Ensure your dog is up to date on vaccinations.
  • Schedule a meet-and-greet before the stay whenever possible.
  • Avoid changing your dog’s diet shortly before boarding.
  • Label all belongings with your dog’s name.
  • Provide an emergency contact in addition to your veterinarian’s information.

Personalized Care at House & Pets Care

Our goal is to provide a safe, calm, and loving environment where your dog feels at home. By sharing detailed information about your pet’s habits and preferences, you help us deliver personalized care and ensure a seamless transition during their stay.
